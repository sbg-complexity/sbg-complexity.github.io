<header class="page-hero wrap"><p class="eyebrow">Website information</p><h1>Data Policy</h1></header>

<article class="legal-content wrap">
  <p class="legal-intro">Data collection is very minimal. Personal data is collected when you voluntarily use the contact form and include details such as your name, email address, or message.</p>

  <section>
    <h2>1. Purpose of Data Collection</h2>
    <p>The contact form prepares an email with the information you provide. Your email application opens so that you can review and send it. We use information you choose to send solely to process your inquiry and communicate with you.</p>
  </section>

  <section>
    <h2>2. Data Security</h2>
    <ul>
      <li>This website is served over HTTPS.</li>
      <li>The contact form does not submit or store information on this website. It opens your email application, and the message is sent only if you choose to send it there.</li>
      <li>Your email and message are handled by the email services used by you and the recipients, under their security and privacy practices.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>

  <section>
    <h2>3. Data Storage</h2>
    <p>Your data is stored only as long as necessary to handle your inquiry, after which it is securely deleted.</p>
  </section>

  <section>
    <h2>4. Sharing with Third Parties</h2>
    <p>Your data will not be shared with third parties, unless you explicitly consent or it is legally required.</p>
  </section>

  <section>
    <h2>5. User Rights</h2>
    <p>You have the right to access, correct, or delete your data at any time. You can contact us through the <a href="{{ '/contact/' | relative_url }}">Contact page</a>.</p>
  </section>
</article>
